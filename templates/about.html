THE SELF-TAUGHT GRIND

Everything changed at age 13. Armed with my dad's laptop, I locked myself in and taught myself the intricate systems of modern music production. In that exact same year, I picked up a guitar and completely taught myself how to play, merging live strings with electronic structures.

My journey started with heavy, rhythmic trap beats, sharing my developments directly with listeners on my YouTube channel. That foundational era taught me the science of crisp drum design, heavy low-frequency management, and competitive arrangement techniques.

Now, at 19 years old (and turning 20 next year), that foundation supports full-length lofi albums streaming live on Spotify. The objective is clear: network, elevate the composition quality daily, and break into the industry's premier curated playlists.

SONIC INSPIRATIONS

The production language of Xavier Beats is built on the pillars of legendary hip-hop mentors, YouTube tech-creators, and underground chillout curators:

FOUNDATIONAL HITMAKERS

Timbaland & Simon Servida. Masters of rhythmic unexpectedness and complex bouncing structures. They shaped my understanding of how to make drums dance and pop within a clean sonic stereo image.

MODERN PRODUCTION CULTURE

Prod by Ocean & Kyle Beats. The driving forces that demystified the creative process. Their structural formatting and entrepreneurial drive showed me how an upcoming producer can establish a definitive digital footprint from their bedroom.

THE LOFI TRANSITION

L.dre & Mondo Loops. The direct catalysts who opened my ears to the cozy world of chill lofi beats. Their brilliant blends of jazzy, nostalgic atmospheric chords and vinyl warmth made me pivot toward creating relaxing chillout albums.

THE ACOUSTIC FRAMEWORK

Self-Taught Strings. My deep love for the guitar since childhood acts as my secret weapon. Infusing organic, self-played guitar melodies straight into my digital layouts keeps the music human, soulful, and distinct.
